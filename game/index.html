SOLMAR VICE

Port of Thieves — an original open-world crime story in the sun-soaked city of Puerto Solmar.

You are Rico Marín, a dock runner fresh off the boat. Work for harbour fixer La Gata, steal rides, dodge the Policía Portuaria, and build your name from the marina to the old town.

W A S D / Arrows — walk & drive
Shift — run / boost steering grip
E — enter / exit a car (walk up to any parked car to steal it)
Mouse — aim  •  Click — shoot  •  F — melee
Space — handbrake  •  P / Esc — pause  •  M — mute

Sound starts when you press a button. Progress saves automatically.
